## Early prototype selections

1. **Letter as doorway:** combine #1 and #7 with an original modular type system; a single letter controls what the reader can see behind it.
2. **Pressure map:** combine the construction logic of #24 and #31; ingredient intensity changes a readable radial poem layout.
3. **Chorus poster:** combine #4, #5, and #28; many small marks resolve into a face or sentence without using the saved artwork.

These are design directions for the separate next version. They do not alter the live site.
