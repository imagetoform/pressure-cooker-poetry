# Mexican Muralism & Rasquache: cutouts to find or make

A shopping list, not images. Nothing here is loaded by the site until a real file exists.
The emotion tags are a first guess; when you have the actual picture, tag it by what it feels like (the lab tagger, `?lab=1#/lab`).
Write down where each one came from (scan, collection, your own photo or drawing) and its rights before it goes live.

Where to look: José Guadalupe Posada's broadsides and calaveras (he died 1913; Library of Congress, Met, Art Institute of Chicago); anonymous 19th-century retablos and ex-votos. The murals and the Taller de Gráfica Popular (founded 1937) are still under copyright: make rasquache textures (corrugated metal, painted signs, stucco) as your own photos.
