# Mexican Muralism & Rasquache

1920s on. After the Revolution, Rivera, Orozco and Siqueiros painted workers and Indigenous history on public walls for people who couldn't buy paintings. Rasquache, the Chicano art of making do (named by Tomás Ybarra-Frausto, 1989): rescued materials, loud colour, hand-painted signs.

## Where the public-domain material is

José Guadalupe Posada's broadsides and calaveras (he died 1913; Library of Congress, Met, Art Institute of Chicago); anonymous 19th-century retablos and ex-votos. The murals and the Taller de Gráfica Popular (founded 1937) are still under copyright: make rasquache textures (corrugated metal, painted signs, stucco) as your own photos.
