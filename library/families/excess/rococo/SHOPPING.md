# Rococo: cutouts to find or make

A shopping list, not images. Nothing here is loaded by the site until a real file exists.
The emotion tags are a first guess; when you have the actual picture, tag it by what it feels like (the lab tagger, `?lab=1#/lab`).
Write down where each one came from (scan, collection, your own photo or drawing) and its rights before it goes live.

Where to look: 18th-century ornament engravings (Meissonier, Cuvilliés, Lajoue, Pillement) and prints after Boucher and Fragonard. The Met and the Rijksmuseum release these as CC0.
