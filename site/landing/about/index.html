Multiplying possibilities.

Not a machine for producing answers. A machine for multiplying possibilities.

Pressure Cooker Poetry is a space for generative art, poetry, essays and learning. Its cut-up, punk and Dada spirit invites curiosity, creative agency and collective possibility.

Human flourishing is the destination. Intellectual independence is the method. You are free to challenge our arguments. There is no compulsory worldview or membership, and optimism does not replace material needs or remove structural barriers.

Nothing here needs an account. What you make in Brain Candy stays in your browser unless you copy it out.
