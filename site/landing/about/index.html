The person and the purpose

About & Mission

Pressure Cooker Poetry is an independent creative project built around poetry, art, research, and the conviction that curiosity should not come with a cover charge.

About the creator

I'm an artist, poet, musician, and curious amateur drawn to the places where language, history, politics, design and human imagination collide. This is a workshop in public, not a marble pedestal.

The work here experiments openly. Some things are polished; others are still becoming.

The mission

Make knowledge approachable, art participatory and ideas worth arguing with. Explore the history of propaganda and power, investigate strange connections, and put creative tools in ordinary hands.

No intellectual bouncer at the door. You can disagree, learn something, and make your own damn thing.
