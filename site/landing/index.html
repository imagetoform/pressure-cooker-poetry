The Gutter Intelligentsia

You walked in hungry. Good.

Everyone’s selling you a fake feast: gold foil, 300% markup, nothing inside. Not here. Poetry was never theirs to gatekeep. It’s yours, it’s free, and every seat at this table is equal. Nobody’s handing you answers. Grab what you crave, burn the menu, and draw your own damn map.

Make something → Black out a presidential speech. Cut a page straight through the middle of a word. Fold two texts into one. Free tools from Burroughs, Cage, the Oulipo and the Dadaists, on a workbench in your browser. “Cut ups are for everyone.” W. S. Burroughs

The ivory tower keeps its knowledge behind tuition and jargon. This place carries it back down to the gutter, where people can use it. Linguistics, propaganda, psychology, history with the citations showing, and poetry at the center, because poetry sharpens a mind and belongs to no nation.

The mission is to give people their voices back, and hand over the tools and guides without the gatekeeping. A place for wisdom to call home in its own language.
