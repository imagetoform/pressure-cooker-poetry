Our principles
Tool 001 / from stuck to curious

A question you can actually use.

Name a situation, belief, or obstacle. Keep it as specific as you can. This page does not analyze you or send your words anywhere.

A working prompt, not an answer

Start by distinguishing the situation itself from the story told about why it cannot change.

Privacy: your entry stays in this browser tab; it is not stored or transmitted by this page. No emotional diagnosis, personality score, or automatic persuasion.

Tool 002 / intellectual first aid

Four questions for any claim.

A

What exactly is claimed?

Rewrite it plainly. Separate facts from predictions and moral judgments.

B

What's the evidence?

Seek primary sources, dates, missing context, and independent corroboration.

C

What's the strongest objection?

Make the best opposing case before you decide. Include objections to our own essays.

D

What would change your mind?

Name evidence that would lead you to revise your view, then be willing to revise it.

What we're building toward

Tools, not commandments.

Human flourishing is the destination. Intellectual independence is the method. Collective possibility is the horizon.

Material conditions matter. No amount of positive thinking substitutes for housing, food, safety, time, or meaningful support. We offer exercises and resources, not a promise that individual attitude can fix structural problems.

Disagreement is welcome. We will be wrong about things. The point is to make claims examinable, not to recruit people into a new orthodoxy.
