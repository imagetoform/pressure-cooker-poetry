# PRESSURE COOKER POETRY — GENERATIVE DESIGN DOCTRINE D-42
Source of truth: docs/CANON.md. This is the current visual directive. Old DESIGN.md versions are in docs/history, not production targets.

## What the references mean
The owner's three Cutup Database images and the supplied Cosmos, Pinterest, Are.na, and personal archive material establish the expected sophistication of layered, masked, type-led, immersive editorial design. They DO NOT establish a permanent card grid, cutup database, beige or black foundation, fixed palette, or a homepage made of a random poem. They are input evidence, not the desired constant output.
Honor owner-supplied visual evidence in ALL families, including composition, interactive affordances, visual grammar, color relationships, typography, masked assets, texture, UI and motion, subject to source rights and provenance.

## What the renderer must decide per seed
1. emotional associations, contradictory tensions and family weights from visitor responses;
2. selection and hierarchy of compatible and contrasting design-family kits;
3. full-page composition grammar: asymmetric/lateral, inverted, newspaper/archival, typographic monument, collage organism, immersive layered scene, or new grammars;
4. section grid, negative space, foreground/background graphics, image masks, cropping, depth, typography scales/weights;
5. palette relationships and texture treatment;
6. navigation, doors, cards, buttons, control geometries and section transitions;
7. each image's motion response, while preserving the same interaction contract in every edition;
8. mobile/tablet layout as an authored rearrangement, not shrink-to-fit desktop.

The renderer must generate materially different *structures* for different seeds, not just recolor and reposition one generic layout. Do not add zigzags/dots as filler. The graphic composition must use real approved cutouts. Fixed content destinations remain findable.

## Fixed behavior
Questionnaire first; no generated splash before answers. Animated loading after final answer. Fully generated homepage after loading. All worlds retain Brain Candies, My Two Cents, Contact, Mission Statement and Learning Curve. All worlds include interactive art and an eye that links to /the-eye/. Local opt-in audio only. Reduced motion and keyboard touch support.

## Acceptance
Review at least six different seeds, including contrast in answer sets and at 320px, 390px, 768px, 1280px. Reject visual sameness, repeated structural skeletons, clashing visual layering that hides navigation, unattributed inputs or dead controls. 'Passes tests' does not equal 'artistically approved'.

Current implementation has limited layout grammar diversity: the aim is a durable generative *system*, not a promise that today's code already achieves unlimited variation.
